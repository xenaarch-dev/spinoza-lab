01 · The problem

Every company wants AI.
The serious ones don't trust the cloud.

Banks, manufacturers, hospitals, pharma, law firms and defence suppliers sit on their most valuable asset: private data. Sending it to ChatGPT means sending it to someone else's computer. So they either ban AI and fall behind, or pay huge per-seat subscriptions and hope nothing leaks.

2023

Samsung banned ChatGPT

Engineers pasted internal source code into ChatGPT. Samsung then banned generative AI on company devices. One paste, and the secret is on someone else's server.

Source: Bloomberg, May 2023
₹250 cr

DPDP Act penalty

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Rules were notified in November 2025. Failing to protect personal data can cost up to ₹250 crore per breach, with core duties live by May 2027.

Source: PIB / MeitY, Nov 2025
₹1 cr+

A year, for 500 seats

Microsoft 365 Copilot's business add-on lists at ₹1,745 per user per month in India. For 500 employees that is over ₹1 crore every year, forever, and the data still leaves the building.

Source: Microsoft India pricing, Nov 2026
Why now

AI is no longer optional.
Neither is security.

Three forces meet in 2026: every company now needs AI to stay competitive, free open models have become good enough to run in-house, and cyber attacks are rising every year.

AI

Why AI is a necessity

AI reads, writes, searches and summarises in seconds what takes people hours. Companies that use it move faster and spend less. Companies that ban it fall behind. The question is no longer "should we use AI?" but "whose computer does our data go to?"

3M

Open models, free to own

Hugging Face, the GitHub of AI, now hosts about 3 million public models. Llama, Qwen, Gemma and Mistral can be downloaded once and run anywhere, with no subscription. Spinoza turns that open library into a safe, ready-to-use product for a company.

Source: Hugging Face Hub count, Aug 2026
29.4 L

Cyber incidents in a year

CERT-In handled over 29.44 lakh cyber incidents in 2025, up from 10.29 lakh in 2022. Every prompt sent to an outside server is one more door to guard. Air-gapped AI closes that door.

Source: PIB / CERT-In, Jan 2026

03 · How it works

Everything happens inside the line.

An employee asks a question. The Trust Layer checks who they are, masks personal data and writes a sealed log entry. The local model answers using the company's own files. The internet cable is not part of the picture.
